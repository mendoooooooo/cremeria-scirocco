Granita Sicilian-style

One of the most requested items at Cremeria Scirocco. A granita that honours Sicilian tradition, born from a trip to Sicily.

Just like in Sicily

We don’t use flavoured fruit syrups, but lots of fresh fruit, over 50%, low-sodium natural mineral water and sugars. No secrets for a granita that respects Sicilian tradition.

You can enjoy them with or without our special whipped cream, whipped naturally without added air. And they’re perfect with our brioche.

The “Sciroccata”

For the greediest: granita + a scoop of gelato + whipped cream on top.

Want to order?

Call or write to us: we’ll help you choose flavours and quantities.
