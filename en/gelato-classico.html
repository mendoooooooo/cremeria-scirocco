Our classic flavours

Simple ingredients: local milk, fresh cream, fresh eggs and real fruit. No ready-made bases, no shortcuts.

Simple ingredients

When the season allows, we use raw milk from a small farm near the shop (zero-km milk), which follows strict breeding and milking standards. The rest of the year we still use high-quality milk and cream.

Milk, cream and eggs are then pasteurised in-house in a latest-generation Carpigiani pasteuriser.

The only ingredients we use are

  • Whole milk (for the creams)
  • Fresh cream
  • Fresh eggs
  • Sugars
  • Bourbon vanilla pods from Madagascar
  • Low-sodium natural mineral water (for all sorbets)
  • As the only thickeners: locust bean gum and xanthan

What we don’t use

  • Hydrogenated fats, which cheaply add body and shelf life at the expense of the consumer’s health.
  • Synthetic emulsifiers (mono- and diglycerides), usually used to make up for the lack of fine ingredients.
  • Artificial colourings
  • Artificial flavourings

Fruit gelato

For all our fruit gelato we choose the sorbet method: only fruit, natural mineral water, sugars and a few grams per kg of natural thickeners. Nothing else.

With a high percentage of fruit (over 50%), no milk or other animal fats, no colourings or flavourings, our gelato brings out the aroma of the fruit and suits the lactose intolerant, vegetarians and vegans, and anyone on a low-fat diet.
